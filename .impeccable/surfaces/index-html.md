# Surface brief: the shop page (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page shop (header, first viewport, catalogue, product viewer, compare, inquiry list, repairs, why-us, contact, map, footer, search, manage panel). Mode: Persuade for the first viewport, Operate from the catalogue down.

Audience and job: the four groups in PRODUCT.md, on phones over weak data. Find it, see the price, reach the right desk or walk in.

Proof on hand: real prices and stock, 11 real product photos, live open and answering status, the separate repair desk, the address with its landmarks. Nothing invented.

Constraints: ~9 KB/s customers, 30 of 41 products without photos, every existing feature and data hook, WCAG AA in both themes, service-worker takeover must reach phones.

Pinned by the user (2026-09-24), overriding the direction roll: the look of the sites in their two reference videos, both families, dark cinematic (1, 2, 4, 12, 14) and light premium (3, 5, 11, 13); all four motions (a real 3D object you can spin, flowing light and glow, scroll animations, floating objects); the HOMCOM H in chrome as the 3D centrepiece.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HOMCOM shown like a product launch: the H in polished chrome, lit in its own stage, with the shop's real stock, prices and live status orbiting it. Refuses the category default of a flat shop template, identical card grid, icon placeholders and stat strip.

OWN-WORLD: Night theme is deep space ink (#06070B to #0C0E16) with an ion violet-to-blue light (#7B6BFF to #4C8DFF) and a warm rim amber (#FFB45A) for offers; Day theme is pale lavender-white (#F3F2F8) with the same ion light at low intensity and violet glass ribbons. Chrome, glass panes with a bright top edge, hairline dividers, one type family (Hanken Grotesk, replacing Mona Sans, which the Impeccable detector lists as overused) from 300 to 700 weight, with equal-width figures for prices, big light-weight display lines, tabular prices. Products without photos become dark or pale glass plates with the brand name set large, never an icon.

STORY: The visitor sees the shop's mark turning in light, understands in one line that this is where genuine laptops, phones and repairs are on Tom Mboya Street, sees whether it is open right now, and taps Directions, WhatsApp or search. Scrolling down, the stock rises into view with prices first, the repair desk has its own number, and the page closes on the map and both lines.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: header (H mark, search, list); full-width stage with the chrome H at about 62% of width, light streaks flowing into it, two or three real product photos floating in glass on its edges; below it the one-line headline at about 2.3rem light weight, the live status pill, and two actions (Directions primary, WhatsApp) within the first screen. Desktop: copy left, the H stage right, floating stock around it.

FORM: Brief-pinned form (the user's two reference videos), fused from the launch-page grammar of sites 1, 2 and 4 with the light product grammar of 3, 5, 11 and 13; seed key 7a693557 (roll overridden by the user's pinned brief).

Signature interaction: drag the chrome H to spin it (inertia, eases back to its idle turn); it tilts toward the pointer on desktop. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out entrances from visible defaults, streaks and float on the compositor, everything still under reduced motion, nothing heavy on lite or slow devices.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
