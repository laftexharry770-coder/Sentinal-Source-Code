Before you start

This page only trades while it is open and on screen. Phones suspend background tabs within seconds, so closing it stops the bot mid-position — open trades keep their stop and target on the broker, but no new entries, trailing or recovery steps happen. For unattended trading, run the MT5 Expert Advisor in MQL5/Experts/Sentinal/ on a PC or VPS instead.
